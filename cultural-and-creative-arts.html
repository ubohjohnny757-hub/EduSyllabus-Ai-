JSS1 Cultural and Creative Arts

1. Meaning and Importance of Art

Notes

Art is a way of expressing ideas, feelings, and experiences through creative activities. Examples include drawing, painting, sculpture, music, drama, and dance.

Importance of art

  • Encourages creativity and imagination.
  • Preserves cultural traditions.
  • Provides entertainment.
  • Can create employment and income.

Revision questions

  1. What is art?
  2. Mention four forms of art.
  3. State three reasons art is important.
2. Elements of Art

Notes

The elements of visual art include:

  • Line: A mark that can be straight, curved, thick, or thin.
  • Shape: A flat enclosed area.
  • Form: An object with three-dimensional qualities.
  • Colour: The appearance of an object based on the light it reflects.
  • Texture: How a surface feels or appears to feel.
  • Space: The area around, between, or within objects.
  • Tone: The lightness or darkness of a colour.

Revision questions

  1. List five elements of art.
  2. Explain the difference between shape and form.
3. Drawing and Colouring

Notes

Drawing is the creation of images using lines, shapes, and tones. Colouring adds colour to a drawing to improve its appearance and communicate ideas.

Materials

  • Pencil and eraser.
  • Drawing paper.
  • Ruler.
  • Crayons, coloured pencils, or paints.

Practical activity

Draw a fruit bowl using simple shapes. Add light and dark tones and colour the fruits carefully.

Revision questions

  1. Name four drawing materials.
  2. Explain two differences between drawing and colouring.
4. Introduction to Music

Notes

Music is an organised combination of sounds and silence. It can be used for worship, celebration, storytelling, education, and entertainment.

Basic musical elements

  • Rhythm: The pattern of sounds and beats.
  • Melody: A sequence of musical notes.
  • Tempo: The speed of music.
  • Dynamics: How loud or soft music is.

Revision questions

  1. What is music?
  2. Define rhythm and tempo.
  3. Mention three uses of music.
5. Introduction to Drama and Dance

Notes

Drama presents stories through acting, dialogue, movement, and performance. Dance uses organised body movements to express feelings, ideas, and cultural traditions.

Important terms

  • Actor: A person who performs a role.
  • Audience: People watching a performance.
  • Stage: The area where a performance takes place.
  • Choreography: The arrangement of dance movements.

Revision questions

  1. What is drama?
  2. Define choreography.
  3. State two differences between drama and dance.

JSS2 Cultural and Creative Arts

1. Principles of Art and Design

Notes

Principles of design guide how visual elements are arranged.

  • Balance: Distribution of visual weight.
  • Contrast: Noticeable differences between elements.
  • Unity: A sense that parts belong together.
  • Emphasis: Making one part stand out.
  • Pattern: Repetition of elements.
  • Proportion: The size relationship between parts.

Revision questions

  1. What is balance in art?
  2. List four principles of design.
  3. Explain contrast using an example.
2. Nigerian Traditional Arts and Crafts

Notes

Traditional arts and crafts reflect the history, skills, beliefs, and creativity of Nigerian communities.

Examples

  • Pottery and ceramic work.
  • Weaving and textile design.
  • Bead making.
  • Wood carving.
  • Basket making.
  • Metalwork.

Traditional crafts can preserve cultural knowledge and provide livelihoods for artists and craft workers.

Revision questions

  1. Name five traditional crafts found in Nigeria.
  2. Give two benefits of traditional crafts.
3. Musical Instruments

Notes

Musical instruments produce sound in different ways.

  • Drums: Produce sound when struck.
  • Flutes: Produce sound from vibrating air.
  • String instruments: Produce sound from vibrating strings.
  • Shakers: Produce sound when shaken.

Nigerian musical traditions include instruments such as talking drums, shekere, udu, and various flutes.

Revision questions

  1. Name four musical instruments.
  2. How does a drum produce sound?
  3. Identify two instruments used in Nigerian music.
4. Costume, Make-up and Stage Design

Notes

  • Costume: Clothing worn by performers to represent a character or role.
  • Make-up: Materials applied to help create a character's appearance.
  • Stage design: The arrangement of scenery, props, and other visual elements for a performance.
  • Props: Objects used by performers during a production.

Revision questions

  1. What is a costume?
  2. Explain two uses of props.
  3. Why is stage design important?
5. Dance Patterns and Choreography

Notes

Choreography involves planning and arranging movements for a dance. Dancers may use formations, levels, directions, rhythm, and coordinated movements.

Practical activity

In a safe open space, work with classmates to create a short dance using repeated movements and a simple formation.

Revision questions

  1. Define choreography.
  2. Mention three features of a dance pattern.

JSS3 Cultural and Creative Arts

1. Art Appreciation and Criticism

Notes

Art appreciation means observing and understanding a work of art. Art criticism involves describing, analysing, interpreting, and evaluating the work thoughtfully.

Questions to ask when studying art

  • What can you see in the artwork?
  • Which materials and techniques were used?
  • What message or feeling might the artist communicate?
  • How are the elements and principles arranged?

Revision questions

  1. What is art appreciation?
  2. List four questions used when analysing an artwork.
2. Textile Design and Printing

Notes

Textile design involves creating patterns and designs for fabric. Printing transfers designs onto a surface using methods such as block printing, stencil printing, or screen printing.

Materials

  • Fabric or suitable paper.
  • Approved paints or fabric colours.
  • Stencils or prepared printing blocks.
  • Protective covering for the work area.

Revision questions

  1. What is textile design?
  2. Name three methods of printing designs.
3. Script Writing and Stage Performance

Notes

A script is a written plan for a performance. It usually includes characters, dialogue, actions, and stage directions.

Basic stages of a performance

  1. Develop an idea and storyline.
  2. Create characters and write dialogue.
  3. Assign roles and rehearse.
  4. Prepare costumes, props, and the stage.
  5. Perform and evaluate the presentation.

Revision questions

  1. What is a script?
  2. Identify four elements of a script.
  3. Outline the stages of preparing a drama.
4. Nigerian Cultural Heritage and Festivals

Notes

Cultural heritage includes traditions, artistic works, historical places, music, dances, crafts, and knowledge passed from one generation to another.

Festivals may celebrate harvests, history, religious occasions, community identity, or important events. Their meanings and practices vary among communities.

Revision questions

  1. What is cultural heritage?
  2. Give three examples of Nigerian cultural festivals.
  3. Why should cultural heritage be preserved?
5. Art, Entrepreneurship and Careers

Notes

Creative skills can support self-employment and careers. Artists can produce useful objects, perform, teach, design, or provide creative services.

Possible careers

  • Graphic designer.
  • Painter or sculptor.
  • Fashion or textile designer.
  • Musician or music teacher.
  • Actor or stage designer.
  • Craft maker or art teacher.

Revision questions

  1. Explain how art can create employment.
  2. List five careers related to creative arts.

Revision and Practical Activities

  • Practise drawing and simple designs.
  • Listen to different kinds of music and identify their rhythms.
  • Study examples of Nigerian traditional arts and crafts.
  • Write a short drama script with classmates.
  • Answer each lesson's revision questions in your notebook.
