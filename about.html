About EduSyllabus AI

Your learning companion for school curricula, educational resources, practice quizzes and AI-assisted learning.

Who We Are

EduSyllabus AI is an educational website designed to help secondary school students and teachers access learning resources in one place. Our goal is to make studying, teaching and revision easier, more organised and more accessible.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support students in understanding their subjects, help teachers organise learning materials and encourage students to practise regularly in preparation for examinations.

Who Can Use EduSyllabus AI?

EduSyllabus AI is designed for junior and senior secondary school students and teachers.

A Message to Students
