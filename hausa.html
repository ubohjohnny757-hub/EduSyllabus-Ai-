Barka da zuwa darasin Hausa

Harshen Hausa yana daga cikin manyan harsunan da ake magana da su a Najeriya da wasu ƙasashe. Wannan darasi yana taimaka wa ɗalibai su koyi magana, karatu, rubutu, nahawu, da al'adun Hausawa.

How to use this page: Tap any topic heading to open its lesson notes and revision questions.

JSS1 Hausa Language

1. Haruffan Hausa — Hausa Alphabet

Haruffa su ne alamomin da ake amfani da su wajen rubuta kalmomi.

Hausa uses familiar Latin letters and special characters such as:

Ɓ, Ɗ, Ƙ, and Ƴ.

Examples of Hausa words: ƙofa, ɗalibi, ɓera, and ƴa.

Tambayoyi:

  1. Menene haruffa?
  2. Rubuta haruffan Hausa guda biyar.
  3. Rubuta kalmomi uku masu amfani da haruffa na musamman.
2. Gaisuwa — Greetings

Gaisuwa hanya ce ta nuna girmamawa da kyakkyawar mu'amala.

  • Ina kwana? — Good morning / How was the night?
  • Lafiya lau. — I am fine.
  • Barka da rana. — Good afternoon.
  • Barka da yamma. — Good evening.
  • Sannu. — Hello / Well done, depending on context.
  • Na gode. — Thank you.
  • Don Allah. — Please.
  • Yi haƙuri. — Sorry / Please be patient.

Tambaya: Rubuta gaisuwa guda huɗu da ma'anarsu a Turanci.

3. Lambobi — Numbers
  • Ɗaya — One
  • Biyu — Two
  • Uku — Three
  • Huɗu — Four
  • Biyar — Five
  • Shida — Six
  • Bakwai — Seven
  • Takwas — Eight
  • Tara — Nine
  • Goma — Ten

Tambaya: Rubuta lambobin Hausa daga ɗaya zuwa goma.

4. Iyali — Family

Iyali su ne mutanen da dangantaka ta haɗa tare.

  • Uba — Father
  • Uwa — Mother
  • Ɗa — Son
  • Yarinya — Girl
  • Ɗan'uwa — Sibling / relative
  • Kaka — Grandparent

Tambaya: Ambaci mutane biyar da suke cikin iyali.

5. Sunaye — Nouns

Suna kalma ce da ake amfani da ita wajen kiran mutum, dabba, wuri, ko abu.

  • Gida — House
  • Littafi — Book
  • Mutum — Person
  • Kare — Dog
  • Bishiya — Tree
  • Makaranta — School

Tambaya: Menene suna? Ba da misalai huɗu.

6. Aikatau — Verbs

Aikatau kalma ce da ke nuna aiki ko abin da ake yi.

  • Ci — Eat
  • Sha — Drink
  • Karanta — Read
  • Rubuta — Write
  • Gudu — Run
  • Barci — Sleep
  • Tafi — Go

Tambaya: Menene aikatau? Rubuta aikatau guda biyar.

7. Jimloli Masu Sauƙi — Simple Sentences

Jimla ita ce haɗin kalmomi da ke bayyana cikakkiyar ma'ana.

  • Ali yana karanta littafi. — Ali is reading a book.
  • Ina son makaranta ta. — I love my school.
  • Yara suna wasa. — The children are playing.
  • Mahaifiyata tana girki. — My mother is cooking.

Tambaya: Rubuta jimloli uku a Hausa.

8. Karatun Fahimta — Reading Comprehension

Iyalina

Sunana Amina. Ni ɗaliba ce. Ina da uba, uwa, da 'yan'uwa. Muna zama a gida ɗaya. Mahaifiyata tana koya mana kyawawan ɗabi'u. Muna taimakon juna.

Tambayoyi

  1. Menene sunan ɗalibar?
  2. Waɗanne mutane ne take da su a iyalinta?
  3. Me mahaifiyarta take koya musu?
9. Al'adun Hausawa — Hausa Culture

Al'ada ita ce hanyar rayuwa, imani, da ayyukan da al'umma ke riƙewa daga tsara zuwa tsara.

Misalai sun haɗa da gaisuwa, karin magana, tatsuniya, abinci, kiɗa, rawa, da bukukuwan gargajiya.

Tambaya: Ambaci al'adun Hausawa guda huɗu.

10. Tambayoyin Bita — JSS1 Revision
  1. Menene haruffa?
  2. Rubuta gaisuwa guda huɗu.
  3. Rubuta lambobi daga ɗaya zuwa goma.
  4. Ambaci mutane biyar a cikin iyali.
  5. Menene suna?
  6. Menene aikatau?
  7. Rubuta jimloli uku a Hausa.
  8. Menene al'ada?

JSS2 Hausa Language

1. Wakilan Suna — Pronouns

Wakilin suna kalma ce da ke maye gurbin suna.

  • Ni — I
  • Kai — You (masculine, singular)
  • Ke — You (feminine, singular)
  • Shi — He / it
  • Ita — She / it
  • Mu — We
  • Ku — You (plural or respectful)
  • Su — They

Tambaya: Menene wakilin suna? Ba da misalai huɗu.

2. Lokutan Aiki — Verb Tenses

Lokacin aiki yana nuna lokacin da abu ya faru.

  • Na tafi makaranta jiya. — I went to school yesterday.
  • Ina zuwa makaranta yanzu. — I am going to school now.
  • Zan tafi makaranta gobe. — I will go to school tomorrow.

Tambaya: Rubuta jimla game da abin da ya faru jiya, abin da ke faruwa yanzu, da abin da zai faru gobe.

3. Gabatar da Kai — Self-Introduction

Za ka iya amfani da waɗannan jimloli wajen gabatar da kanka:

  • Sunana Musa. — My name is Musa.
  • Ni ɗalibi ne. — I am a male student.
  • Ina zaune a Najeriya. — I live in Nigeria.
  • Ina son karatu. — I like studying.

Aiki: Rubuta ɗan sakin layi game da kanka a Hausa.

4. Karin Magana — Proverbs

Karin magana kalmomi ne masu ɗauke da hikima da darasi.

Hannu ɗaya ba ya ɗaukar jinka.

Meaning: One hand cannot lift a heavy load alone. It teaches the value of cooperation.

Tambaya: Menene karin magana? Bayyana ma'anar karin maganar da ke sama.

5. Rubutun Ƙirƙira — Composition Writing

Rubutun ƙirƙira yana bayyana tunani cikin tsari mai kyau.

  • Fara da gabatarwar batu.
  • Bayyana muhimman bayanai a tsakiyar rubutu.
  • Yi amfani da jimloli masu sauƙin fahimta.
  • Rufe rubutun da kyakkyawan ƙarshe.

Aiki: Rubuta game da makarantar ka.

6. Tatsuniya — Folktales

Tatsuniya labari ce ta gargajiya da ake amfani da ita wajen koyar da hikima da kyawawan ɗabi'u.

Tatsuniya na iya koyar da haƙuri, gaskiya, haɗin kai, da ladabi.

Aiki: Ba da tatsuniya da ka sani, sannan ka bayyana darasin da ke cikinta.

7. Fassara — Translation

Fassara ita ce bayyana ma'anar magana daga harshe ɗaya zuwa wani harshe.

  • Good morning. — Ina kwana?
  • Thank you. — Na gode.
  • I am a student. — Ni ɗalibi ne / Ni ɗaliba ce.
  • I love my school. — Ina son makaranta ta.

Aiki: Fassara “My name is Fatima” da “I love my mother” zuwa Hausa.

8. Tambayoyin Bita — JSS2 Revision
  1. Menene wakilin suna?
  2. Bayyana lokutan aiki uku.
  3. Rubuta sakin layi game da kanka.
  4. Menene karin magana?
  5. Rubuta game da makarantar ka.
  6. Menene tatsuniya?
  7. Menene fassara?

JSS3 Hausa Language

1. Sautin Hausa — Hausa Sounds

Harshen Hausa yana da sautuka daban-daban. Wasu haruffa suna da sautuka na musamman, kamar Ɓ, Ɗ, da Ƙ.

Fahimtar sautuka na taimakawa wajen furta kalmomi da rubuta su daidai.

Aiki: Rubuta kalmomi biyar masu amfani da haruffan Ɓ, Ɗ, ko Ƙ.

2. Nau'ikan Jimloli — Types of Sentences

Jimloli na iya bayyana bayani, tambaya, umarni, ko mamaki.

  • Ali yana karatu. — Statement.
  • Ina za ka? — Question.
  • Don Allah, zauna. — Request or instruction.
  • Kai! Wannan abin mamaki ne! — Exclamation.

Tambaya: Rubuta misali guda ɗaya na kowane nau'in jimla huɗu.

3. Tattaunawa — Dialogue

Tattaunawa ita ce musayar magana tsakanin mutane biyu ko fiye.

Ali: Ina kwana, Musa?

Musa: Lafiya lau. Kai fa?

Ali: Lafiya lau. Me kake yi?

Musa: Ina karanta littafi.

Aiki: Rubuta tattaunawa tsakanin ɗalibai biyu game da makaranta.

4. Rubutun Muhawara — Argumentative Writing

Rubutun muhawara yana bayyana dalilan goyon baya ko ƙin wani ra'ayi.

Ya kamata ya ƙunshi gabatarwa, hujjoji, da ƙarshe.

Aiki: Rubuta muhawara a kan batu: “Ilimi yana da muhimmanci ga kowane ɗalibi.”

5. Tarihi da Al'umma — History and Society

Tarihi yana taimaka mana fahimtar abubuwan da suka faru a baya da yadda al'umma ta ci gaba.

Yana da muhimmanci a bincika bayanai da kyau kuma a kwatanta tushe daban-daban.

Aiki: Tambayi dattijo game da tarihin garinku, sannan ka rubuta abin da ka koya.

6. Al'adu da Bukukuwan Hausawa

Al'adun Hausawa sun haɗa da harshe, abinci, sutura, kiɗa, rawa, karin magana, tatsuniya, da bukukuwan gargajiya.

Al'adu na iya bambanta daga wuri zuwa wuri. Yana da muhimmanci a fahimce su kuma a girmama bambance-bambance.

Tambaya: Bayyana abubuwa huɗu da ke cikin al'adun Hausawa.

7. Karatun Fahimta — Reading Comprehension

Karatun fahimta yana nufin karanta rubutu, fahimtar ma'anarsa, da amsa tambayoyi game da shi.

  • Gano babban batu.
  • Gano muhimman bayanai.
  • Fahimtar ma'anar kalmomi daga mahallinsu.
  • Amsa tambayoyi bisa abin da aka karanta.

Aiki: Karanta wani ɗan labari a littafinka, sannan ka rubuta babban sakonsa.

8. Tambayoyin Bita — JSS3 Revision
  1. Ambaci haruffan Hausa uku na musamman.
  2. Rubuta nau'ikan jimloli huɗu.
  3. Rubuta tattaunawa tsakanin mutane biyu.
  4. Menene rubutun muhawara?
  5. Me ya sa tarihi yake da muhimmanci?
  6. Ambaci abubuwa huɗu na al'adun Hausawa.
  7. Menene karatun fahimta?

Ƙarshen Darasi — End of Curriculum

Karanta darussan, rubuta muhimman bayanai, sannan ka amsa tambayoyin bita don gwada fahimtarka.
