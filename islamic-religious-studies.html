JSS1 Islamic Religious Studies

1. Meaning and Importance of Islam

Learning objectives

Understand the meaning of Islam and its importance to Muslims.

Notes

Islam is a religion based on belief in and submission to Allah. Muslims learn to worship Allah, practise good character, help others, and live responsibly.

Review questions

  1. What is Islam?
  2. Mention three ways Muslims can practise good character.
2. The Five Pillars of Islam

Notes

The Five Pillars are the main acts of worship in Islam:

  1. Shahadah — declaration of faith.
  2. Salah — the five daily prayers.
  3. Zakah — obligatory charity for eligible Muslims.
  4. Sawm — fasting during Ramadan.
  5. Hajj — pilgrimage to Makkah for those able to perform it.

Review questions

  1. List the Five Pillars of Islam.
  2. Explain the importance of Salah.
3. The Qur'an

Notes

The Qur'an is the holy book of Islam. Muslims believe it is the revelation of Allah to Prophet Muhammad through Angel Jibril. It teaches faith, worship, honesty, justice, mercy, and good conduct.

Review questions

  1. What is the Qur'an?
  2. Mention two lessons Muslims learn from the Qur'an.
4. Prophet Muhammad (Peace Be Upon Him)

Notes

Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) is regarded by Muslims as the final prophet. His life provides examples of honesty, patience, kindness, trustworthiness, and forgiveness.

Review questions

  1. Who is Prophet Muhammad in Islam?
  2. Mention three good qualities associated with his life.
5. Cleanliness and Personal Hygiene

Notes

Cleanliness is important in Islamic practice. Muslims are taught to maintain personal hygiene, keep their surroundings clean, and prepare properly for worship.

Review questions

  1. Why is cleanliness important in Islam?
  2. Give three examples of good hygiene.

JSS2 Islamic Religious Studies

1. The Six Articles of Faith

Notes

The six commonly taught articles of Islamic faith concern belief in:

  1. Allah.
  2. Angels.
  3. Revealed books.
  4. Prophets and messengers.
  5. The Last Day.
  6. Divine decree (Qadar).

Review questions

  1. List the six articles of faith.
  2. What is meant by belief in prophets?
2. Salah and Its Importance

Notes

Salah is a central act of worship performed at prescribed times. It encourages discipline, remembrance of Allah, humility, and regular spiritual reflection.

Review questions

  1. How many obligatory daily prayers are there?
  2. Give three benefits of Salah.
3. Fasting During Ramadan

Notes

Sawm during Ramadan involves abstaining from food, drink, and other things that invalidate the fast from dawn to sunset. Fasting teaches self-discipline, patience, gratitude, and concern for people in need. Islamic law recognises exemptions in certain circumstances.

Review questions

  1. What is Sawm?
  2. Mention three lessons of fasting.
4. Zakah and Charity

Notes

Zakah is an obligatory form of giving that applies to eligible Muslims who meet the relevant conditions. Voluntary charity is also encouraged. Giving supports people in need and promotes social responsibility.

Review questions

  1. What is Zakah?
  2. How can charity benefit a community?
5. Honesty, Respect and Good Character

Notes

Islamic teachings encourage truthfulness, respect for parents and elders, kindness to neighbours, keeping promises, and fair treatment of others.

Review questions

  1. Give four examples of good character.
  2. Why is honesty important in society?

JSS3 Islamic Religious Studies

1. Hajj and Its Significance

Notes

Hajj is the pilgrimage to Makkah performed during its appointed period by Muslims who are physically and financially able. It promotes devotion, unity, equality, patience, and sacrifice.

Review questions

  1. What is Hajj?
  2. Mention three lessons of Hajj.
2. The Life and Teachings of Selected Prophets

Notes

Islamic teachings recognise many prophets, including Adam, Nuh (Noah), Ibrahim (Abraham), Musa (Moses), and Isa (Jesus), peace be upon them. Their accounts teach faith, obedience, patience, courage, and trust in Allah.

Review questions

  1. Name four prophets recognised in Islam.
  2. Mention two lessons from the stories of the prophets.
3. Islamic Brotherhood and Community Life

Notes

Islam encourages mutual support, justice, peaceful relations, care for neighbours, and assistance to people in need. Muslims are taught to avoid harmful behaviour and resolve disagreements fairly.

Review questions

  1. Explain Islamic brotherhood.
  2. Give three ways students can promote peace at school.
4. Islamic Teachings on Peace and Justice

Notes

Justice means treating people fairly and respecting their rights. Islamic moral teachings encourage peaceful conduct, honesty, reconciliation, and avoiding oppression.

Review questions

  1. What is justice?
  2. Mention three ways to promote peace in a community.
5. Accountability and the Hereafter

Notes

Muslims believe in life after death and accountability before Allah. This belief encourages responsible choices, good deeds, honesty, and consideration for the consequences of one's actions.

Review questions

  1. What does accountability mean?
  2. How can belief in the Hereafter influence behaviour?
