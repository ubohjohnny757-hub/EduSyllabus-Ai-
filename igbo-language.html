Welcome to Igbo Language

Asụsụ Igbo bụ otu n'ime asụsụ ndị a na-asụ na Naịjirịa. Igbo Language helps students learn to speak, read, write, understand, and appreciate Igbo language and culture.

How to use this page: Tap any lesson heading to open the notes and revision questions.

JSS1 Igbo Language

1. Mkpụrụedemede Igbo — Igbo Alphabet

Mkpụrụedemede bụ akara ndị e ji ede okwu.

The Igbo alphabet includes letters such as:

A, B, CH, D, E, F, G, GB, GH, GW, H, I, Ị, J, K, KP, KW, L, M, N, Ṅ, NW, NY, O, Ọ, P, R, S, SH, T, U, Ụ, V, W, Y, Z

Igbo also uses special letters such as Ị, Ọ, and Ụ.

Ajụjụ:

  1. Gịnị bụ mkpụrụedemede?
  2. Kpọọ mkpụrụedemede Igbo ise.
  3. Kpọọ mkpụrụedemede Igbo atọ nwere ntụpọ n'okpuru.
2. Ekele — Greetings

Ekele bụ okwu e ji egosi nkwanye ùgwù na ịhụnanya.

  • Ụtụtụ ọma — Good morning.
  • Ehihie ọma — Good afternoon.
  • Mgbede ọma — Good evening.
  • Kedu? — How are you?
  • Adị m mma — I am fine.
  • Daalụ — Thank you.
  • Ndo — Sorry.

Ajụjụ: Kọwaa ekele anọ n'asụsụ Igbo.

3. Onụọgụgụ — Numbers

Examples of Igbo numbers:

  • Otu — One
  • Abụọ — Two
  • Atọ — Three
  • Anọ — Four
  • Ise — Five
  • Isii — Six
  • Asaa — Seven
  • Asatọ — Eight
  • Itoolu — Nine
  • Iri — Ten

Ajụjụ: Dee ọnụọgụgụ Igbo site n'otu ruo iri.

4. Ezinụlọ — Family

Ezinụlọ bụ ndị mmadụ jikọrọ ọnụ dịka nne, nna na ụmụ ha.

  • Nne — Mother
  • Nna — Father
  • Nwanne — Sibling
  • Nwa — Child
  • Nne ochie — Grandmother
  • Nna nna — Grandfather

Ajụjụ: Kpọọ ndị ezinụlọ ise n'asụsụ Igbo.

5. Aha — Nouns

Aha bụ okwu e ji akpọ mmadụ, anụmanụ, ebe, ma ọ bụ ihe.

Nouns name people, animals, places, and things.

  • Ụlọ — House
  • Akwụkwọ — Book
  • Nwaanyị — Woman
  • Nwoke — Man
  • Ewu — Goat
  • Osisi — Tree

Ajụjụ: Gịnị bụ aha? Nye ihe atụ anọ.

6. Ngwaa — Verbs

Ngwaa bụ okwu na-egosi ihe mmadụ ma ọ bụ anụmanụ na-eme.

  • Rie — Eat
  • Ṅụọ — Drink
  • Gụọ — Read
  • Dee — Write
  • Gbaa ọsọ — Run
  • Rụọ ọrụ — Work

Ajụjụ: Gịnị bụ ngwaa? Kpọọ ngwaa ise.

7. Ahịrịokwu Dị Mfe — Simple Sentences

Ahịrịokwu bụ okwu e jikọtara ọnụ iji wepụta echiche zuru oke.

  • Ada na-agụ akwụkwọ. — Ada is reading.
  • Ụmụaka na-egwu egwu. — The children are playing.
  • Nna m na-arụ ọrụ. — My father is working.
  • Amaka na-aṅụ mmiri. — Amaka is drinking water.

Ajụjụ: Dee ahịrịokwu Igbo atọ.

8. Agụmagụ — Reading Practice

Ezinụlọ M

Aha m bụ Ada. Ezinụlọ m nwere nna m, nne m na ụmụnne m. Anyị bi n'ụlọ dị mma. Nne m na-akụziri anyị ezi omume. Anyị na-enyere ibe anyị aka.

Ajụjụ

  1. Gịnị bụ aha nwa agbọghọ ahụ?
  2. Ole ndị ka o kwuru banyere ha n'ezinụlọ ya?
  3. Gịnị ka nne ya na-akụziri ha?
9. Omenala Igbo — Igbo Culture

Omenala bụ ụzọ ndụ, nkwenkwe, na omume ndị obodo na-ebufe site n'ọgbọ ruo n'ọgbọ.

Examples include traditional greetings, storytelling, music, dance, food, and community celebrations.

Ọ dị mkpa ịkwanyere ndị mmadụ na omenala ha ùgwù.

Ajụjụ: Kpọọ ihe atụ omenala Igbo anọ.

JSS2 Igbo Language

1. Nnọchiaha — Pronouns

Nnọchiaha bụ okwu na-anọchi aha mmadụ ma ọ bụ ihe.

  • Mụ — I/me
  • Gị — You
  • Ya — He, she, or it
  • Anyị — We/us
  • Unu — You (plural)
  • Ha — They/them

Ajụjụ: Gịnị bụ nnọchiaha? Nye ihe atụ anọ.

2. Oge Ngwaa — Verb Tenses

Oge ngwaa na-egosi mgbe ihe mere, na-eme, ma ọ bụ ga-eme.

  • Emere — Past: Ihe mere n'oge gara aga.
  • Na-eme — Present: Ihe na-eme ugbu a.
  • Ga-eme — Future: Ihe ga-eme n'ọdịnihu.

Ana m aga ụlọ akwụkwọ. — I am going to school.

Agara m ụlọ akwụkwọ. — I went to school.

Mgbe e mesịrị, aga m ụlọ akwụkwọ. — Later, I will go to school.

Ajụjụ: Kọwaa oge gara aga na oge ugbu a.

3. Nkọwa Onwe Onye — Self-Introduction

Ị nwere ike iji ahịrịokwu ndị a kọwaa onwe gị:

  • Aha m bụ Chika. — My name is Chika.
  • Abụ m nwa akwụkwọ. — I am a student.
  • Amụrụ m na Naịjirịa. — I was born in Nigeria.
  • Ụlọ akwụkwọ m dị n'obodo m. — My school is in my town.

Ọrụ: Dee paragraf dị mkpirikpi gbasara onwe gị n'asụsụ Igbo.

4. Ilu Igbo — Igbo Proverbs

Ilu bụ okwu amamihe a na-eji akụzi ezi omume ma ọ bụ kọwaa ndụ.

Otu osisi anaghị eme ọhịa.

Meaning: One tree does not make a forest. People often need one another to achieve great things.

Ajụjụ: Gịnị bụ ilu? Kọwaa ihe ilu dị n'elu pụtara.

5. Edemede — Composition Writing

Edemede bụ ide echiche n'usoro doro anya.

Mgbe ị na-ede edemede:

  • Malite site n'okwu mmeghe.
  • Kọwaa isiokwu n'ahụ edemede.
  • Jiri ahịrịokwu doro anya.
  • Mechie edemede gị nke ọma.

Ọrụ: Dee edemede gbasara ụlọ akwụkwọ gị.

6. Akụkọ Ifo — Folktales

Akụkọ ifo bụ akụkọ ọdịnala ndị mmadụ na-akọ iji kụziere ụmụaka na ndị okenye ihe.

Akụkọ ifo nwere ike ịkụzi ezi omume, ndidi, ịdị n'otu, na amamihe.

Ọrụ: Kọọ akụkọ ifo ị maara ma kọwaa ihe ọ na-akụzi.

7. Ntụgharị Asụsụ — Translation

Ntụgharị asụsụ bụ ịkọwa ihe e kwuru n'otu asụsụ n'asụsụ ọzọ, ebe a na-echekwa ihe ọ pụtara.

  • Good morning. — Ụtụtụ ọma.
  • Thank you. — Daalụ.
  • I am a student. — Abụ m nwa akwụkwọ.
  • My mother is kind. — Nne m dị obiọma.

Ọrụ: Tụgharịa ahịrịokwu ndị a n'asụsụ Igbo: “My name is Ada” na “I love my school.”

JSS3 Igbo Language

1. Ntụpọume na Ụdaolu — Tone Marks

Ụdaolu bụ otú olu si arị elu ma ọ bụ daa mgbe a na-ekwu okwu. N'asụsụ Igbo, ụda olu nwere ike ịgbanwe ihe okwu pụtara.

Ntụpọume na akara ụdaolu na-enyere onye na-agụ akwụkwọ aka ịmata otú e si akpọ ụfọdụ okwu.

Ọrụ: Gụọ ihe atụ onye nkuzi nyere ma gee ntị n'otú ụda si agbanwe.

2. Ụdị Ahịrịokwu — Types of Sentences

Ahịrịokwu nwere ike ịkọwa ihe, ịjụ ajụjụ, inye iwu, ma ọ bụ igosi mmetụta.

  • Ada na-agụ akwụkwọ. — Statement.
  • Ị na-aga ebee? — Question.
  • Biko, mechie ụzọ. — Request or instruction.
  • Ewoo! Nke a dị mma! — Exclamation.

Ajụjụ: Dee otu ihe atụ nke ụdị ahịrịokwu anọ ndị a.

3. Mkparịtaụka — Dialogue

Mkparịtaụka bụ mkparịta okwu dị n'etiti mmadụ abụọ ma ọ bụ karịa.

Ada: Ụtụtụ ọma, Chika.

Chika: Ụtụtụ ọma, Ada. Kedu?

Ada: Adị m mma. Gịnị ka ị na-eme?

Chika: Ana m agụ akwụkwọ.

Ọrụ: Dee mkparịtaụka n'etiti ụmụ akwụkwọ abụọ gbasara ụlọ akwụkwọ.

4. Edemede Arụmụka — Argumentative Writing

Edemede arụmụka bụ edemede a na-ede iji kwado ma ọ bụ jụ echiche site n'inye ihe kpatara ya.

Edemede kwesịrị inwe okwu mmeghe, isi ihe nwere ihe akaebe ma ọ bụ ihe atụ, na nkwubi okwu.

Ọrụ: Dee edemede gbasara isiokwu a: “Ịgụ akwụkwọ dị mkpa nye ụmụ akwụkwọ.”

5. Akụkọ Akụkọala — Traditional History

Akụkọala na-enyere anyị ịmụta banyere ndụ ndị gara aga, mmalite obodo, na ihe ndị dị mkpa n'ọdịbendị.

Ọ dị mkpa iji nlezianya nyochaa akụkọ, tụnyere isi mmalite, ma kwanyere ndị mmadụ ùgwù.

Ọrụ: Jụọ onye okenye gbasara akụkọ obodo unu ma dee isi ihe ndị ị mụtara.

6. Ọdịbendị na Emume Igbo

Ọdịbendị gụnyere asụsụ, nri, egwu, ịgba egwu, uwe, akụkọ ọdịnala, na emume obodo.

Ụfọdụ emume dị iche iche site n'otu obodo gaa n'ọzọ. Ndị mmadụ kwesịrị ịmụ ọdịbendị nke ọma ma kwanyere ọdịiche dị iche iche ùgwù.

Ajụjụ: Kọwaa akụkụ anọ nke ọdịbendị Igbo.

7. Ntụgharị Asụsụ na Nghọta Agụmagụ

Nghọta agụmagụ bụ ịgụ ederede, ịchọpụta isi echiche ya, na ịza ajụjụ gbasara ihe a gụrụ.

Mgbe ị na-agụ ederede:

  • Chọpụta isiokwu.
  • Chọta isi echiche.
  • Chọpụta ihe omume na ndị sonyere na ya.
  • Zaa ajụjụ site n'ihe ederede kwuru.

Ọrụ: Gụọ akụkọ dị mkpirikpi n'akwụkwọ gị ma dee isi echiche ya.

Ozi Ikpeazụ — Final Note

Gụọ ihe ọmụmụ ndị a, dee ihe ndị dị mkpa n'akwụkwọ gị, ma zaa ajụjụ nyocha iji nwalee nghọta gị.
