About Physical Education

Physical Education is the study and practice of movement, exercise, games, sports, and physical activities. It helps students develop fitness, coordination, teamwork, and discipline.

JSS1 Physical Education

1. Meaning and Importance of Physical Education

Physical Education teaches people how to use their bodies through exercise, games, movement, and sports.

Importance

  • Improves fitness and coordination.
  • Develops teamwork and discipline.
  • Encourages healthy habits.
  • Provides enjoyment and recreation.

Revision: Define Physical Education and list three benefits.

2. Physical Fitness

Physical fitness is the ability to perform everyday activities effectively without becoming excessively tired.

Important components include strength, endurance, flexibility, speed, agility, and balance.

Revision: List five components of physical fitness.

3. Fundamental Movement Skills

These are basic movements used in games, sports, exercise, and daily activities.

  • Walking and running.
  • Jumping and hopping.
  • Throwing and catching.
  • Kicking and striking.
  • Balancing and changing direction.

Revision: Name four fundamental movement skills.

4. Warm-Up and Cool-Down

A warm-up prepares the body for exercise. A cool-down allows the body to return gradually to its resting state.

Both should be appropriate to the activity. Movements and stretches should be comfortable and controlled.

Revision: Explain the difference between a warm-up and a cool-down.

5. Athletics

Athletics consists of running, jumping, and throwing events.

  • Track events: sprinting and distance running.
  • Jumping events: long jump and high jump.
  • Throwing events: shot put and javelin.

Revision: Give two examples each of track and field events.

6. Personal Hygiene in Sports
  • Wear clean sportswear.
  • Wash hands after physical activities.
  • Keep personal equipment clean.
  • Drink safe water.
  • Clean and cover minor wounds appropriately and tell a responsible adult about injuries.

Revision: State four hygiene rules for sports participants.

7. Safety Rules During Exercise
  • Check that the playing area is safe.
  • Warm up before vigorous activity.
  • Use equipment correctly.
  • Wear suitable footwear.
  • Follow the teacher's instructions.
  • Report injuries immediately.

Revision: List five safety rules for physical activities.

8. Recreation and Leisure

Recreation refers to activities people choose during their free time for enjoyment, relaxation, or personal development.

Examples include walking, dancing, playing games, and participating in supervised sports.

Revision: Define recreation and give four examples.

JSS2 Physical Education

1. Principles of Physical Fitness

Fitness improves through suitable and regular exercise.

  • Regularity: exercise consistently.
  • Progression: increase activity gradually.
  • Specificity: practise activities related to your fitness goal.
  • Recovery: allow the body enough rest.

Revision: Explain progression and recovery.

2. Team Games

Team games involve players working together toward a shared goal.

Examples include football, basketball, volleyball, and handball.

Players should cooperate, communicate, follow the rules, and respect opponents and officials.

Revision: Name four team games and explain two benefits of teamwork.

3. Football Skills

Football is a team game in which players try to score goals against their opponents.

Basic skills include passing, dribbling, shooting, trapping, and defending.

Players must follow the rules and the referee's decisions.

Revision: List five basic football skills.

4. Volleyball Skills

Volleyball is played by two teams separated by a net. Teams try to send the ball into the opposing team's court.

Basic skills include serving, passing, setting, attacking, and blocking.

Revision: Explain three volleyball skills.

5. First Aid in Sports

First aid is the immediate assistance given to an injured or suddenly ill person before professional medical care is available.

  • Ensure the area is safe.
  • Inform a teacher or responsible adult.
  • Seek emergency medical help for serious injuries.
  • Do not move someone if a serious neck or back injury is suspected unless there is immediate danger.
  • Never give food or drink to an unconscious person.

Revision: Define first aid and state three appropriate actions after a sports injury.

6. Sportsmanship

Sportsmanship means behaving fairly and respectfully during games and sports.

It includes obeying rules, respecting officials, cooperating with teammates, and accepting results fairly.

Revision: List four qualities of good sportsmanship.

7. Posture and Body Movement

Posture is the way the body is held while sitting, standing, or moving.

Good habits include using a comfortable sitting position, carrying suitable loads, and changing position regularly during long periods of sitting.

Revision: Define posture and give three healthy body-care habits.

JSS3 Physical Education

1. Physical Fitness Training

Fitness training is planned physical activity used to improve fitness and sports performance.

  • Continuous training: steady activity over a period of time.
  • Interval training: alternating activity and recovery.
  • Circuit training: completing exercises at different stations.
  • Flexibility training: exercises that maintain or improve joint movement.

Exercise should be suitable for the student and increased gradually.

Revision: Describe three fitness training methods.

2. Athletics and Competition

Athletics includes track and field events.

Competitors need preparation, suitable technique, fair play, and knowledge of the event rules.

Officials help ensure that competitions are conducted fairly and safely.

Revision: Give three examples of athletics events.

3. Recreation and Healthy Living

Recreational activities help people enjoy free time, relax, and remain active.

Examples include dancing, walking, gardening, and playing games.

Healthy living also involves sufficient sleep, balanced meals, hygiene, and appropriate physical activity.

Revision: State four benefits of recreational activities.

4. Sports Injuries and Prevention

Sports injuries may happen during training or competition.

Prevention includes warming up, using suitable equipment, following rules, practising correct techniques, and allowing adequate recovery.

Report injuries to a teacher or trained first aider. Serious injuries require prompt medical attention.

Revision: List five ways to prevent sports injuries.

5. Environmental Health and Sanitation

Environmental sanitation means keeping surroundings clean and reducing conditions that may cause illness.

  • Dispose of rubbish properly.
  • Keep toilets and drains clean.
  • Protect drinking water from contamination.
  • Keep classrooms and playing fields free of hazards.
  • Reduce stagnant water where mosquitoes can breed.

Revision: State five ways to maintain environmental sanitation.

6. Leadership in Sports

Sports leadership is the ability to guide and encourage others during physical activities and competitions.

A good leader communicates clearly, follows rules, encourages teamwork, and treats everyone fairly.

Revision: Mention five qualities of a good sports leader.

7. Stress Management and Wellbeing

Wellbeing includes physical, emotional, and social health.

Healthy habits include sleeping sufficiently, taking breaks, staying connected with supportive people, enjoying recreational activities, and speaking with a trusted adult when help is needed.

Revision: List four healthy ways to manage everyday stress.
