JSS1 French

1. Greetings and Introductions

Vocabulary

  • Bonjour — Good morning / Hello
  • Bonsoir — Good evening
  • Salut — Hi / Bye (informal)
  • Au revoir — Goodbye
  • Merci — Thank you
  • S'il vous plaît — Please (polite)

Example conversation

A: Bonjour ! Comment tu t'appelles ?

B: Je m'appelle Amira. Et toi ?

Meaning: Hello! What is your name? My name is Amira. And you?

Revision questions

  1. What does Bonjour mean?
  2. How do you say “Thank you” in French?
  3. Write a short French greeting.

3. The French Alphabet

Notes

The French alphabet has 26 basic letters, just like the English alphabet. However, some letters are pronounced differently. French also uses accents, such as é, è, ê, and ç.

Examples

  • École — School
  • Élève — Student
  • Français — French

Revision questions

  1. How many basic letters are in the French alphabet?
  2. Write two French words containing accents.

2. Colours

Vocabulary

  • Rouge — Red
  • Bleu — Blue
  • Vert — Green
  • Jaune — Yellow
  • Noir — Black
  • Blanc — White
  • Orange — Orange
  • Rose — Pink

Some French colour adjectives change their endings to agree with the noun they describe. For example, “vert” becomes “verte” with a feminine noun.

Revision questions

  1. Translate “rouge” and “jaune”.
  2. Write five colours in French.
3. Articles: Un, Une, Le and La

Notes

  • Un — a/an, masculine
  • Une — a/an, feminine
  • Le — the, masculine
  • La — the, feminine
  • Les — the, plural

Examples

  • Un livre — A book
  • Une table — A table
  • Le garçon — The boy
  • La fille — The girl
  • Les élèves — The students

Revision questions

  1. Which article is commonly used with a feminine singular noun?
  2. Translate “un livre”.
  3. Write one sentence using “la”.
4. Common Verbs: Être and Avoir

Être — To be

  • Je suis — I am
  • Tu es — You are (informal)
  • Il/Elle est — He/She is
  • Nous sommes — We are
  • Vous êtes — You are (formal/plural)
  • Ils/Elles sont — They are

Avoir — To have

  • J'ai — I have
  • Tu as — You have (informal)
  • Il/Elle a — He/She has
  • Nous avons — We have
  • Vous avez — You have (formal/plural)
  • Ils/Elles ont — They have

Revision questions

  1. What does “je suis” mean?
  2. Translate “nous avons”.
  3. Complete: Elle ___ élève. (être)

  2. Write three sentences about your daily activities in French.
2. Forming Questions and Negative Sentences

Asking questions

French questions can be formed with question words, rising intonation, or structures such as “est-ce que”.

  • Comment ? — How?
  • Où ? — Where?
  • Quand ? — When?
  • Pourquoi ? — Why?
  • Est-ce que tu étudies ? — Do you study?

Negative sentences

A common French negative structure places “ne” before the conjugated verb and “pas” after it.

Je parle français. — I speak French.
Je ne parle pas français. — I do not speak French.

Revision questions

  1. Translate “Où ?” and “Pourquoi ?”.
  2. Change “Je parle français” into a negative sentence.
3. Shopping and Everyday Conversation

Useful expressions

  • Combien ça coûte ? — How much does it cost?
  • Je voudrais... — I would like...
  • C'est trop cher. — It is too expensive.
  • Merci beaucoup. — Thank you very much.
  • Au revoir. — Goodbye.

Example conversation

A: Bonjour ! Combien ça coûte ?

B: Ça coûte cinq euros.

A: Merci beaucoup.

Meaning: Hello! How much does it cost? It costs five euros. Thank you very much.

Revision questions

  1. How do you ask the price of an item?
  2. Write a short conversation between a buyer and a seller in French.
4. Writing a Short Paragraph in French

Example paragraph

Bonjour ! Je m'appelle Amira. Je suis élève. J'habite au Nigeria. J'aime lire et étudier le français.

Meaning: Hello! My name is Amira. I am a student. I live in Nigeria. I like reading and studying French.

Writing tips

  • Begin sentences with a capital letter.
  • Use appropriate French accents.
  • Check spelling and verb forms.
  • Practise introducing yourself and your family.

Revision questions

  1. Write five sentences introducing yourself in French.
  2. Translate your paragraph into English.
5. Reading Comprehension

Read the passage

Je m'appelle Paul. J'ai quatorze ans. J'habite à Lagos. Je suis élève dans un collège. J'aime les mathématiques, le français et le football.

Meaning: My name is Paul. I am fourteen years old. I live in Lagos. I am a student in a school. I like Mathematics, French, and football.

Questions

  1. Comment s'appelle le garçon ?
  2. Quel âge a Paul ?
  3. Où habite-t-il ?
  4. Qu'est-ce qu'il aime ?

French Revision Tips

  • Learn a few new French words every day.
  • Read the words aloud to practise pronunciation.
  • Practise writing short sentences and conversations.
  • Answer the revision questions after each lesson.
